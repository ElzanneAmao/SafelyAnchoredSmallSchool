Welcome to Safely Anchored Small School

Safely Anchored is an independent Christian primary school in Sunningdale, Cape Town. Our journey began in 2015 as a dedicated aftercare, and today we extend that same heart-centered care to learners from age 4 through to Grade 7.

We meet our families where they are and walk alongside them. Our approach is rooted in today, knowing there is beauty and wisdom in each phase of development. When content and care are driven with intention, children truly learn to love learning.

"Nurturing growth in a secure, loving environment where every child is known by name."
